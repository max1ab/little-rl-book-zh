High-entropy tokens RLVR training mostly impacts high-entropy tokens (tokens for which the
model is uncertain) [Wang et al. 2025]. These usually
represent forking tokens in the reasoning path like
“therefore”, “perhaps” or “however”.

boost the impact of RL on these tokens. Most consist
in raising the upper clipping ratio to allow for more
aggressive updates on these tokens (DAPO, JustRL,
Lite-PPO, MiniRL). Others limit the update on these
tokens. These strategies also help slow the entropy
decrease of the policy.
Length normalization The original GRPO
objective weights down each trajectory gradient
by its length ( |y1i | ), thereby weighting every token
equally between the trajectories. For some (DAPO,
Dr. GRPO), this is an error carried from supervised
learning and has no reason to be here as it biases
answers toward shorter positive trajectories or long
negative ones. Others argue that it is a useful bias to
have to promote long answers and thus encourage
reasoning chains to emerge.
Reward shaping An active line of work introduces explicit length-related reward terms, motivated
by the observation that reasoning models tend to
over-expand their CoT even on trivial prompts [Chen
et al. 2025]. The simplest schemes apply a constant
penalty on the length of the response. Applying such
penalty too early or too strongly tends to collapse
exploration and accuracy. More refined schemes like
Cursor Composer 2 adapt the length penalty to the
task difficulty, so the model is forced to be concise
on easy prompts but can think for longer when the
