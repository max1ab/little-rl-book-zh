Between MC and TD

So far we have seen two extremes: MC, which uses
the full return Gt , and TD, which uses a one-step
bootstrap Rt+1 + γV (St+1 ). They sit at opposite
ends of a spectrum, with opposite trade-offs: MC
has high variance but no bias, TD has low variance
but introduces bias through bootstrapping. Can we
interpolate between the two and get the best of both
worlds?

However, a single n is rarely optimal throughout.
The best choice depends both on the state and on the
progress of training:
• Spatially, states whose neighbors have accurate value estimates can use a small n, since the
bootstrap V (St+n ) is trustworthy, but states
surrounded by poorly estimated values need a
larger n to look past the biased bootstrap and
reach real reward.

About the λ parameter:
• λ = 0 gives TD(0) (what we called TD up until

• low values of λ give more weight to short-term

• high values of λ give more weight to long-term
