Dynamic sampling Some setups (DAPO,
INTELLECT-3) ensure that training prompts
produce a useful learning signal. This can be
done within a batch (filtering out prompts whose
rollouts are all correct or all incorrect), or over a
longer horizon (curriculum-style sampling that
progressively favors harder prompts).
